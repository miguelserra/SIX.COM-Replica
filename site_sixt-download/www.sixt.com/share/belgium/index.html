SIXT share in Belgium

Explore all SIXT share services available in Belgium. All services can be found and booked via the SIXT app. Please note, that all mobility partners have different pricing models and rates. SIXT share partners with felyx in Belgium providing moped sharing service in a numerous cities. Moped sharing is bookable 24/7 via the SIXT app. The journeys must end within the service area indicated in the SIXT app. All mopeds can be found directly in the SIXT app while booking the service.
