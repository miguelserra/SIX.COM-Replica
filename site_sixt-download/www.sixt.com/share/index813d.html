WHERE IS SIXT SHARE AVAILABLE?

You can find SIXT share in Germany, as well as in the Netherlands, France, Spain, Italy and Belgium. Find out where you can find us on the respective country pages or directly in the SIXT app. You only have to register once and can use all of SIXT services, no matter where you are.

SIXT share is a mobility service that offers a flexible mobility services: from bikes, mopeds, scooters to shared cars. You can book shared car from a minute up to 7 days. Simply book a vehicle in the business area with your smartphone, open it and drive off. All vehicles can be found in the SIXT app.

To use SIXT share, you always need the latest version of the SIXT app. There you can see all available vehicles in the business area. You can reserve these for up to 15 minutes free of charge via the app and drive off straight away. You can end the rental within the SIXT share business area for free or at one of our 500 SIXT stations across Germany for a fee.
