CAR SHARING BERLIN AIRPORT

Whether you’re in Berlin for business, on holiday, or returning from a long journey, SIXT share provides convenient and affordable car sharing at Berlin Airport (Berlin Brandenburg). Available 24/7 and bookable via the SIXT app, you can choose car sharing from 1 minute to several days, including one-way trips. Car sharing is a perfect solution for those who want a freedom of driving themselves from Berlin airport to city center.

Please note that pick-up and drop-off fees apply for car sharing at airport. Parking is available only in designated SIXT share area, ensuring a hassle-free experience at the airport.

PARKING AREA - BERLIN BRANDENBURG AIRPORT

CAR SHARING TO THE BERLIN AIRPORT

  • Follow the signs P2-P8.
  • Turn right into "Schönefelder Allee" which will change into "Hugo-Junkers-Ring".
  • The street will lead you to the Bus-parking.
  • After the Bus-parking turn left to P4, here you will find specially marked SIXT share parking spaces.
  • The barrier to the entrance opens automatically.

CAR SHARING FROM THE BERLIN AIRPORT TO CENTER

  • Follow the signs for P4-P6
  • Simply drive through the barrier to the exit. The gate will open automatically
  • Follow the street "Hugo-Junkers-Ring" which will change into "Schönefelder Allee"
