Choose the Right Car Rental Category for You

At SIXT, we offer a diverse and dynamic fleet that can cater to your rental needs no matter what the drive requires. Choose from our economy options or from our specialty fleet of luxury cars that includes models from leading manufacturers such as BMW, Mercedes- Benz, Audi, and Cadillac among others. Minivans and passengers vans are available for group travel and we have vans and trucks for transporting cargo or commercial use. At selected branches you can rent a cargo van, pickup or moving truck to take care of a variety of transportation tasks. We try our best to satisfy your mobility needs, however our fleet varies from location to location and some vehicle types are may not available at every branch. In the unlikely event we cannot provide you with a specific car rental, you will be guaranteed another model from the same car category (ACRISS code).
